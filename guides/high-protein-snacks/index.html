High-Protein Snacks: 25 Easy Ideas With 15 to 30 g Protein

The easiest way to hit your protein target is a smart snack or two. Here are 25 ideas with real numbers.

By Timur Leontyev, Founder of Increvio
Updated November 8, 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Most active adults need about 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kg per day (Morton et al., 2018). Snacks make that much easier.
  • Aim for 15 to 30 g of protein per snack so it counts as a real protein serving.
  • Dairy (skyr, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, quark) is the easiest high-protein snack base.
  • Protein before bed (like quark or cottage cheese) can support overnight muscle building (Snijders et al., 2019).

Why protein snacks help

Three meals often aren't enough to reach a high protein target comfortably. Muscle building also seems to work best when protein is spread over several servings of about 0.4 g/kg each (Schoenfeld & Aragon, 2018). A protein snack between meals or before bed solves both problems. And because protein is filling, it helps with hunger in a diet.

Sweet snacks

  • Skyr with berries and a little honey (about 25 g protein)
  • Quark with cocoa and banana, like a pudding (about 30 g)
  • Protein oats overnight: oats, milk, skyr, a scoop of protein (about 35 g)
  • Cottage cheese with pineapple (about 24 g)
  • Protein shake blended with frozen berries (about 25 g)
  • Rice cakes with quark and jam (about 15 g)

Savory snacks

  • Hard-boiled eggs, 3 eggs (about 19 g)
  • Tuna on whole-grain crackers (about 28 g)
  • Cottage cheese with cucumber, salt and pepper (about 24 g)
  • Turkey or chicken slices rolled with cheese (about 20 g)
  • Hummus with veggie sticks plus a cheese stick (about 15 g)
  • Smoked salmon on rye bread (about 18 g)
  • Leftover chicken from meal prep (100 g, about 30 g)

On the go

  • Protein bar (check for at least 15 to 20 g protein)
  • Ready-to-drink shake
  • Jerky
  • A pack of roasted edamame or chickpeas
  • Small cups of skyr from any supermarket

Before bed

A slowly digested protein like the casein in quark or cottage cheese, about 30 to 40 g before sleep, has been shown to support overnight muscle protein synthesis after training (Snijders et al., 2019). Quark with a few berries is a classic.

Watch out for

  • Protein bars that are really candy bars. Check that protein is at least as high as sugar.
  • Nuts as a "protein snack." Nuts are healthy but mostly fat: 30 g of almonds has about 6 g protein and 180 kcal.
  • Too many shakes. Fine as a backup, but whole foods are more filling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best high-protein snack?

For most people, skyr, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese or quark: cheap, easy, 20 to 30 g protein per serving, and available everywhere.

Are protein snacks good for weight loss?

Yes. They help you hit your protein target and keep you full, which makes a calorie deficit easier.

Can I eat protein snacks every day?

Yes. Protein from varied whole-food snacks is part of a normal healthy diet for most people. People with kidney disease should talk to a doctor about protein intake.
