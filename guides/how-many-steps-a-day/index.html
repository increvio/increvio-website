How Many Steps a Day Do You Really Need?

The 10,000-step goal came from marketing. Here's what large studies actually found.

By Timur Leontyev, Founder of Increvio
Updated November 8, 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • In a meta-analysis of 15 studies, more daily steps were linked to lower risk of death, with benefits leveling off around 6,000 to 8,000 steps for adults 60+ and 8,000 to 10,000 for younger adults (Paluch et al., 2022).
  • About 8,000 steps a day is a great everyday target for most people.
  • Steps are an easy way to burn extra calories and fight the slowdown in daily movement that often happens when dieting.
  • Any increase helps: going from 4,000 to 6,000 steps matters.

Where 10,000 steps came from

The 10,000 number is often traced to a Japanese pedometer marketed in the 1960s. It's a round, catchy goal, not a number from research. That doesn't mean it's bad. It just isn't magic, and it isn't a minimum.

What research shows

A 2022 meta-analysis combined 15 studies with almost 50,000 adults (Paluch et al., 2022). The more steps per day, the lower the risk of dying during follow-up, up to a point:

  • Adults 60 and older: benefits leveled off around 6,000 to 8,000 steps.
  • Adults under 60: benefits leveled off around 8,000 to 10,000 steps.

Another large study found that higher step counts were linked to lower mortality, while step intensity mattered less once total steps were accounted for (Saint-Maurice et al., 2020).

Steps and fat loss

Walking burns roughly 30 to 50 kcal per 1,000 steps depending on body weight and pace. Going from 4,000 to 9,000 steps a day can add 150 to 250 kcal of daily burn, without extra fatigue for your training.

Steps also help with something many dieters don't notice: when people eat less, they often move less without realizing. Keeping a step target protects your deficit. See Weight loss plateau.

How to get more steps

  • Walk after meals: 10 minutes after lunch and dinner adds about 2,000 steps
  • Walking calls and meetings
  • Take the stairs
  • Park further away or get off public transport one stop early
  • Walk to the gym if it's close
  • A podcast walk in the evening

Frequently asked questions

Is 8,000 steps a day enough?

For most adults, yes. Research shows most of the health benefit is reached around 6,000 to 10,000 steps depending on age.

Do steps count as exercise?

Steps are physical activity and support health and fat loss. They don't replace strength training for building muscle.

How many steps a day to lose weight?

Steps help, but fat loss depends mainly on your diet. Around 8,000 to 10,000 steps a day adds a meaningful amount of calorie burn for most people.
