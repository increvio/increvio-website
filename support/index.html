Frequently asked questions

What does Increvio cost?

Training, your streak, friends and your path to your dream body are free, plus daily tips, nutrition rings and your meals for today. With Increvio Plus, your coach plans the whole week: meal plan, grocery list, prep guide and unlimited replanning. Plus costs €59.99 per year (€5 a month) or €12.99 monthly. You can try the yearly plan free for 14 days. To get you started, Leo gives you 7 days of Plus without a subscription, and if you invite a friend you both get 30 days of Plus.

How do I cancel Plus?

In your iPhone settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Increvio. Cancel within the first 14 days of the yearly trial and you pay nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, all data stays on your iPhone. You need an account for friends and to restore your plan on a new iPhone. Sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email. Data is stored in Frankfurt (EU).

How do I add friends?

Open the Friends tab and tap the plus button to share your invite link. When your friend opens it, you're connected. If you both join through an invite, you both get 30 days of Plus.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app under Profile, Data & privacy, "Delete account and all data". This deletes your account and all data on the server and on your iPhone.

Which languages does Increvio support?

English and German.
