About the author

Timur Leontyev

Founder of Increvio

Timur Leontyev is the founder of Increvio, a fitness app for people who know what to do but struggle to keep doing it. Increvio plans meals, groceries and training, and uses friends, streaks and a realistic path to help people stay consistent.

How our guides are written

  • Based on research. Every guide is built on peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses and position stands. Sources are listed at the end of each guide, so you can check them yourself.
  • Honest and realistic. No miracle promises, no "six-pack in four weeks". We describe what the evidence supports and where it is uncertain.
  • Written for real life. The focus is on what helps people stay consistent: simple plans, small steps and the people around you.
  • Kept up to date. When new research changes a recommendation, we update the guide and its date.
  • Not medical advice. Guides are general information for healthy adults. If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor.
