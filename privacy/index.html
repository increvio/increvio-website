Privacy Policy

No data protection officer has been appointed, as the legal requirements for this (Art. 37 GDPR, § 38 BDSG) are currently not met.

2. In short

  • Increvio is a wellness app for nutrition, training and consistency. It does not diagnose anything and does not replace medical advice.
  • To create your plan we need health data (e.g. weight, height). We only process it with your explicit consent, which you give in the app and can withdraw at any time.
  • Without an account, all data stays on your iPhone. With an account, your profile is stored on servers in Frankfurt (EU).
  • Friends never see your weight, calories or what you eat. Only check marks, your streak, points and workouts.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no advertising IDs.

3. What data we process

3.1 Data on your iPhone (with and without an account)

Body measurements, progress photos, cycle data and your voice memo ("Your why") stay exclusively on your iPhone: not in your account, not in the backup.

Onboarding answers (goal, experience, sex, date of birth, height, weight, goal weight if provided, daily activity, sports besides strength training, training days and location, food preferences and dislikes, allergies and intolerances if provided, cooking effort, how you heard about Increvio, coach settings), weekly plans, checked-off meals, logged deviations, saved foods with your own nutrition values, water glasses, meal times, workouts (exercises, weights, reps), skipped workouts with reason (e.g. "sick"), other sports and recovery, weight entries, your own supplements, streak and points.

This data lives in the app on your device. Part of it is stored in the app's protected storage for the home screen widget (streak, today's workout, next meal).

Progress photos (optional): photos you select are resized and stored only on your iPhone (protected app folder). Not in your account, not with friends, not with us. The app only sees the photos you pick.

Cycle tracker (optional): period start dates stay exclusively on your iPhone. They are not stored in the account backup or sent to friends or us.

Apple Health (optional): if you connect Apple Health, Increvio reads your workouts, weight, steps and sleep. Increvio does not write anything to Apple Health. Only the following is used: type, date, duration and distance of workouts like running, football or cycling (no strength training, no walking), your body weight (one value per day, only for days without your own entry), today's steps (only shown for the fat loss goal, not stored) and your usual wake-up time if you haven't set one. Your HRV from the last 30 days is only shown on the recovery page, not stored, and never leaves your iPhone. Heart rate, calories, sleep stages and routes are not used. Imported sports are treated like sports you entered yourself (with an account also in your backup, see 3.2). Apple Health data is never used for advertising, never sold and never given to friends or third parties, unless you explicitly allow Leo in the chat to use your data (then your average sleep duration is included, see 3.4c). Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw in the Health app at any time.

Reminders are scheduled on the device. No data is sent to us for this.

Chat history with Leo: stored only on your iPhone (protected app folder, not in the backup), at most 100 messages from the last 30 days. You can delete it in the chat at any time.

3.2 Account (optional)

  • Email address for signing in via link or code (no password).
  • Profile copy, so your plan comes back on a new iPhone: onboarding answers, calorie adjustment and date of the last weight check, settings (coach, reminders, sharing switches), swapped or added exercises, dishes marked "don't like", pantry items, names of your own supplements.
  • Time of your consent to health data processing.
  • Backup of your app data, so nothing is lost with a new phone or deleted app: workouts, weight entries, "ate according to plan" answers, logged sports, skipped workouts with reason and note, weekly plans with checked meals and deviations, custom exercises, sleep answers, streak days repaired with Plus, checked-off supplements and habits from the last 60 days. Stored in Frankfurt (EU), readable only by you (access control at database level), automatic in the background. When you delete your account, the backup is deleted too.
  • Not stored on the server: settings for this device only (e.g. tempo timer).

3.2a Global ranking (optional, account only)

If you join, all users see your chosen nickname, your points from the last 30 days and your level. Never your name, email, weight or food. Leaving deletes your entry immediately. If you report a nickname, we store who reported whom in order to remove offensive names. If you have a profile picture, all participants can see it there too. Reported content is reviewed and removed if it violates the rules. Legal basis: your consent by joining (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

3.2b Challenges (optional, account only)

Title, duration and your daily check marks of a challenge are stored in Frankfurt. For challenges "with friends", your friends see title, participants and check marks. Private challenges are visible only to you. They are deleted with your account.

3.3 Friends (optional, account only)

If you use the friends feature, only people you are friends with see:

  • your first name (freely chosen) and your invite code,
  • your current streak and your points from the last 30 days including your level,
  • feed entries: "workout done" (with the name of the training day), "day on plan", streak milestones, "on the way to the gym" / "training now" (can be turned off, disappears after 2 hours), "skipping training today" (without a reason; nothing is shared for "sick"),
  • likes,
  • your profile picture (optional; stored in the EU, accessible only to signed-in friends),
  • gym moments (optional): workout photos with optional text, visible to friends only, automatically deleted after 7 days. You can report and block at any time.
  • "trained together": who with whom on which day (visible only to the two of you),
  • planned group workouts: title, time, place (optional), participants,
  • in friend groups, all accepted members see your first name, profile picture, streak and when you trained, even if you are not directly friends,
  • achievements you share yourself (confirmed each time): strength gain on an exercise, workouts per month, "goal weight reached" and, only if you explicitly turn it on, your weight change per month (e.g. "−1.5 kg", never your weight itself). You can delete any of your own feed entries.
  • Starter group (optional): up to 6 other new users see only an automatically generated nickname (not your name, no profile picture), your streak and when you trained (last 100 days). You can leave at any time.

Without your active sharing, friends never see weight, height, calories, food or reasons for skipping.

3.3a Discover: public events, chat and inspiration (optional, account only)

Under "Friends → Discover" you can share content that all signed-in users see. Nothing is shared automatically. Public events include title, sport, date and time, place (name and coordinates from Apple Maps search), optionally number of spots and a short text, plus your first name and profile picture. The event chat is visible only to people who joined. Inspiration posts contain photos with optional short text, your first name, profile picture and hearts. Photos are resized on your iPhone, which removes location (GPS) and other metadata, and stored in Frankfurt in non-public storage, accessible only to signed-in users. The app never attaches weight, measurements, calories or similar values.

Location: iOS only asks for your location ("while using") when you open the map. Your location stays on your iPhone and is never sent to or stored by us. Only the visible map area, rounded to about 10 km, is sent to load events. "Add to calendar" opens Apple's calendar dialog; Increvio gets no access to your calendar.

Moderation: you can report events, messages and posts and block people. A report stores who reported what, when and why, with a copy of the content. We review reports and delete content that breaks the rules; serious or repeated violations lead to a ban from these features. From three reports, content is hidden until reviewed. A simple word filter rejects severe insults, and rate limits apply against spam. You can delete your own content at any time; deleting your account deletes everything, including photos.

3.3b Chat with friends and voice messages (optional, account only)

You can exchange text messages (up to 1,000 characters) and voice messages (up to 60 seconds) with friends; nudges appear in the same thread. We store sender, recipient, content or recording, time, when the message was seen and whether someone saved it, at Supabase in Frankfurt, readable only by the two of you. A message is deleted 24 hours after it was seen, unseen messages after 30 days, unless one of you saves it. Reported messages are kept as a copy until reviewed. Push notifications for new messages contain your first name and the text (up to 140 characters), or only "voice message" and its length. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; moderation Art. 6(1)(f).

3.3c Public feed (on by default, can be turned off any time)

"Show in public feed" is turned on with your friends profile. The app tells you explicitly the first time, and you can turn it off immediately or later under Profile → Settings → Coach & Friends. While it is on, all app users see your first name, profile picture and entries from the last three days: workout name, exercise of a personal best (without weight or reps), streak length and workouts per month. Weights, weight changes, body measurements, food and other health values never appear there. Without a profile picture you are not shown. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR; you can object at any time by turning the switch off.

3.4 AI estimate of meals (optional, from the second estimate with an account)

When you type a deviation (e.g. "kebab and a coke"), only this text is sent via our server to the AI service Anthropic (USA), which estimates the nutrition values. No names, weight or profile data are sent. We store when you used the estimate (for a daily limit), not the text. Voice input is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition, on the device where possible; the recording is not stored. You can also send a photo of your meal; it is resized, sent to Anthropic only for the estimate and not stored by us or Anthropic.

Once without an account: you can try the first estimate without an account. The app creates a random identifier for this purpose only. We store it and an irreversible hash of your IP address so the trial can only be used once. Both are deleted after 30 days. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3.4a Import a training plan from a photo (optional, account only)

If you import a plan from a photo or screenshot, we send the image via our server to Anthropic (Claude) to recognize exercises and sets. The image is not stored by us or used to improve models. User ID and profile data are not sent. Please photograph plans only, not people.

3.4b Scan packages for your own recipes (optional)

When you scan a package, the app requests the nutrition values from Open Food Facts (non-profit association, France). Only the barcode number is sent; with Increvio Plus you can also search by name, then only the search term is sent. Open Food Facts technically receives your device's IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Your own recipes and ingredients stay on your iPhone (and in your backup if you have an account).

3.4c Talk to Leo (chat, optional, account and Increvio Plus only)

Messages you send to Leo go via our server (Supabase, Frankfurt) to Anthropic (USA) so Leo can reply. Always included: the latest messages of the conversation, your goal, your daily targets (calories, protein), your plan for today and the rest of this week, and deviations you logged. If you send a recipe link, our server fetches the page and includes the recipe.

Only if you explicitly tap "Allow" in the chat does Leo also get a short summary of your own data: weight trend of the last 8 weeks (weekly averages), sleep duration from Apple Health (if connected, average of the last 4 weeks), your mood (if logged), workouts of the last 4 weeks with sets, weights, reps and personal bests, skipped workouts with reason, other sports, your streak, your meal plan of the week with deviations, and your goals.

No name, email address or user ID is sent to Anthropic, and no data of friends. We store neither the messages nor the summary, only when you used the chat (for a daily limit). Anthropic does not use the data to train its models. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for health data Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR (your explicit consent). You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.

3.5 Anonymous usage statistics (only with consent)

Only if you choose "Yes, sure" in the app do we send anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, e.g. "workout finished", "day checked off", "weight logged" (without the value). No names, no weight, no food. With the same consent, we send once a day whether the app crashed or froze (type of error and app version, no content). You can turn this off in the app.

3.5a Push notifications from friends (optional)

If you allow notifications and have an account, we store your iPhone's push address (a device code issued by Apple) with your account ID, so we can notify you when a friend nudges you, trains, is on the way to the gym or reaches a streak milestone (at most 4 per day, can be turned off). Delivered via the Apple Push Notification service. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. When you sign out, the push address of this iPhone is deleted.

3.5b Find friends via contacts (optional)

If you tap "Match contacts" and allow access, the app reads the email addresses of your contacts on your iPhone and creates irreversible checksums (SHA-256). Only these checksums are sent to our server, compared once with the sign-in addresses of other users and not stored. Names, phone numbers and other contact data never leave your iPhone. Only users who turned on "findable via contacts" are shown. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Inviting contacts opens your iPhone's Messages app; you decide whether and to whom to send.

3.6 Sign-in emails

We use the email service Resend for sign-in emails. Your email address and the content of the sign-in email are processed.

3.6a Witness by email (optional, account only)

You can add a person as a witness. We store their email address and the first name you enter and send them a confirmation email via Resend. Only after they confirm do they get emails (at most one per day): the morning after a skipped or missed planned workout, and on Sundays good news if you completed all planned workouts of the week. The witness only learns "trained / skipped / X of Y workouts", never weight, food, health data or reasons; "sick" and vacation count as rest days. The witness can unsubscribe with one click. If you remove the witness, their address and reported days are deleted immediately; reported days are otherwise deleted after 3 weeks. Unconfirmed invitations expire after 14 days. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for the witness Art. 6(1)(a).

3.7 Technically necessary data

When loading recipes and exercises and with all server requests, technical data (e.g. IP address, time) is processed by the hosting provider for operation and security. These logs are stored briefly and then deleted automatically.

3.7a Increvio Plus subscription (optional)

You buy and manage the subscription through your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment data, name or Apple ID. The app checks with Apple on your iPhone whether a subscription is active and stores locally since when you had Plus. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.8 Website increvio.com

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, time and browser to deliver the page and protect it against attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, fast website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics tools and loads no fonts or content from third parties.

5. How long we keep data

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Old weekly plans are deleted after about 4 weeks.
  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Discover content: until you delete it or your account.
  • Chat with friends: 24 hours after being seen, unseen after 30 days; saved messages until unsaved, the friendship ends or an account is deleted.
  • Feed entries: until you delete your account.
  • Push address: until you sign out, delete your account or Apple reports the app was removed.
  • Usage statistics: anonymous, without reference to you.

6. Delete your account and data, withdraw consent

In the app under Profile → Data & privacy → "Delete account and all data". This deletes your account, profile, friends profile, feed entries and likes, friendships, joint workouts, group workouts, public events, chat messages, inspiration posts with photos, and AI usage timestamps, on the server and on the iPhone. Withdrawing consent to health data is done through this deletion (Increvio cannot create a plan without height and weight). Usage statistics can be turned off separately at any time.

7. Your rights

Access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Requests to support@increvio.com. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, e.g. the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

8. Minimum age

Increvio is for people aged 18 and over. The app asks for your date of birth during onboarding; it cannot be used under 18.

9. No diagnoses

Increvio gives general recommendations based on scientific studies. It does not diagnose, does not recommend medication and does not replace medical advice.

10. Security

Data is only transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Access to server data is restricted per person (row level security; friends data only for confirmed friends). Your sign-in is stored in the iPhone keychain. No AI key is stored in the app, only on the server.

11. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The current German version is at increvio.com/datenschutz.
