The problem

Questions

What is Increvio?

An iPhone fitness app for people who know what to do but struggle to stay consistent. Leo, your coach, plans your meals, grocery list and workouts, replans when life gets in the way, and your friends see whether you show up.

Is Increvio free?

Yes. Training plans, check-offs, your streak, friends and your path to your goal are free forever. Increvio Plus adds the full weekly meal plan, grocery list, prep guide and unlimited replanning.

Who is Increvio for?

Anyone 18 or older who wants to build muscle, lose fat or stay in shape. Beginner or experienced, at the gym or at home.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone. For friends and backups, sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email.

Can my friends see my weight?

No. Friends only see check marks, your streak, points and completed workouts. Weight and meals stay private.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you want. Increvio can read your weight, steps and activities like running or cycling. Health data stays on your iPhone.

Is this a diet app?

No. Increvio is a wellness app. It doesn't diagnose anything and doesn't replace medical advice. Targets are based on established research, not crash diets.

Can I cancel Plus anytime?

Yes, in your iPhone settings under Apple Account, Subscriptions. Cancel within the first 14 days of the yearly plan and you pay nothing.
