How it works

Three minutes to set up. Then Increvio tells you every day what's next.

    Build muscle, lose weight or maintain. Add your body data and training days. Leo asks for the rest along the way.

Questions

When can I download Increvio?

From November 5 on the App Store, for iPhone. Join the waitlist above and you'll get an email with the link on launch day. After that, we delete your address.

Who is Increvio for?

For anyone 18 or older who wants to build muscle, lose weight or stay in shape. Beginner or experienced, at the gym or at home.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone. For friends and backups, sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email.

Can my friends see my weight?

No. Friends only see check marks, your streak, points and completed workouts. Weight and meals stay private unless you choose to share a win yourself.

Does Increvio work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you want. Increvio reads your weight from your scale, your steps and activities like running or cycling. This health data stays on your iPhone.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account, only on your iPhone. With an account, in Frankfurt (EU). Health data only with your explicit consent.

Is this a diet app?

No. Increvio is a wellness app. It doesn't diagnose anything and doesn't replace medical advice. Your targets are based on established research, not crash diets.

Can I cancel Plus anytime?

Yes, with a few taps in your iPhone settings under "Apple Account" and "Subscriptions". Cancel within the first 14 days and you pay nothing.
